– Six students were given the task of ringing bells at the intervals of 10

seconds, 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 50 seconds, 1 minute and 1.25 minutes,
respectively. If they ring bells together at 10 a.m., then how many times will they ring
the bell together in the next 3 hours (including the bell rang at 10 a.m.)?
