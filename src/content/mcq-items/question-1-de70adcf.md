Question is given followed by two statements labelled I and II. Identify

which of the statements is/are sufficient to answer the question.
Questions:_In a refreshment stall six friends – V, W, X, Y , Z and T – were sitting
around a circular table facing towards the centre. Who was sitting to the immediate
right of T?
Statements:-
1. Z was sitting second to the right of V, who was sitting to the immediate left of X. W was
sitting second to the left of X.
2. Y was sitting to the immediate right of Z, who was sitting second to the right of V. W was
sitting second to the left of X who is to the immediate left of Z.
