– 9 men, 7 women and 5 children are to distribute 2483 pens in three

days. On the first day, all were present to distribute. On the second day, 3 men and 2
children were absent. On the third day, 3 women and 3 children were absent. The
number of pens distributed by each man, woman and child in a single day is in the
ratio of 5 : 3 : 2. Calculate the total pens distributed on the third day.
