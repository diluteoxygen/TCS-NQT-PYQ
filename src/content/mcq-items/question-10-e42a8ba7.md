– A statement is given followed by two arguments. Decide which of the

Arguments is/are strong with respect to the statement.
Statement:

In each and every type of graduation, financial education should be made mandatory
in the curriculum.
Arguments:
1. Students having good financial education will lead to development of the economy of the
nation.
2. Financial education will help students to not fall in debt trap.
