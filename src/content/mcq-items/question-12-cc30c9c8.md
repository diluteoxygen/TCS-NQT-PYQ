which of the statements are sufficient to answer the question.

In a reputed school, the number of Grade I, II & III teaching faculty members are in
ratio of 4:5:6 respectively. Find the percentage of female family members in the
school?
Statements:
1. 50% of grade I facility members are male and 30% of grade II faculty members are
female.
2. In grade III, the number of female faculty members is equal to the number of male faculty
members.
