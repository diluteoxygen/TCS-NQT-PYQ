A person distributed 72% of the money he had between Ram and Raj in

the ratio of 5 : 7 respectively. Ram and Raj deposited the amount received in a
scheme offering 8% and 10% simple interest, respectively, for 10 years. Find the
amount in Rs left with the person if the sum of interests earned by Ram and Raj after
10 years is Rs 6,600.
