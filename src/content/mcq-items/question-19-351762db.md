“What is this smell?”

He wrinkles his nose and sniffs the air.

“It is room freshener, sir.” He fishes out a container from one of the side cupboards
and hands it over to me. It is indeed the room freshner. It assures you of a redolence
of wild roses.
“I was happy with dank, friend”.
“Sir?”
“Never mind. Is there another room vacant? I can’t sleep in this room.”

“We have two rooms on the top floor, sir. One of them is vacant. But it is not air-

The night is breezy and pleasant. I light up a mosquito repellent and, in the whirring
