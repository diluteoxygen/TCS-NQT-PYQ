km/h, and returns to Delhi on the same route at the speed of 80 km/h. Another
passenger travels by train from Chandigarh to Kalka at the speed of 60 km/h in time

t, and from Kalka to Solan at the speed of 40 kmh in time t. What is the average
speed of the car and the train, respectively?
