test which had two components, written test (WT) and interview (IV) and one had to
qualify at the WT to go for IV. 20% and 30% respectively, of M and F had qualified at
the WT. Thereafter, 10% and 12% respectively, of M and F qualified. What was the
percentage of the females out of the total number of males that had finally qualified
if the initial number of male and female candidates were equal?
