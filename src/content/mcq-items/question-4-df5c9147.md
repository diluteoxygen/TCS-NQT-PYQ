– In a certain code

P%Q means ‘P is the daughter of Q’
P@Q means ‘P is the son-in-law of Q’
PxQ means ‘Q is the mother of P’
PQ means ‘Q is the sister of P’
P$Q means ‘Q is the son of P’ and
P*Q means ‘Q is the wife of P’
