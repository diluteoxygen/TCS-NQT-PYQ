A questions is given, followed by two statements labelled I and II.

Identify which of the statements is/are sufficient to answer the question.
Questions: In a tutorial, there are six students- Phani, Kiran, Jwalith, Ram, Shyam and
Tony of different ages. Who is the oldest among them?
Statements:
1. Ram is older than Phani and Jwalith. Ram is younger than Kiran. Shyam is older than only
Tony.
2. Shyam is older than Jwalith but younger than Phani. Tony is older than only Ram. Phani
is not the oldest.
