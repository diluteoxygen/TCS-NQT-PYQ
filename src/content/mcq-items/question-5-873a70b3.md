Eight friends namely-H, I, J, K, L, M, N and O-are sitting in a straight line

equidistant from each other (but not necessarily in the same order) Some of them
are facing north, while some are facing south. M sits third to the right of H. M sits at
one of the extreme ends. L sits third to the left of H. Both the immediate neighbours
of J face north. N is not an immediate neighbour of H. The persons sitting at the
extreme ends face the same direction. Both the immediate neighbours of H face the
direction opposite to that of M. Both the immediate neighbours of O face opposite
directions. K is one of the immediate neighbors of L and is facing north, I sits exactly
between J and M. Not more than four persons are facing north L sits to the
immediate right of K. How many persons are sitting between I and O?
