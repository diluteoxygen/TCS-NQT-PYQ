A question is given followed by two statements labelled I and II. Identify which one
of the following statement is/are necessary to answer the question.
Questions:

What will be the height of three students Rachana,Rupali, and Pritam altogether ,if
pritam’s height is 39cm?

Statements:
1. The sum of the height of Rachna & pritam is equal to 160cm.
2. The height of Rupali is equal to 3 times the height of pritam.
