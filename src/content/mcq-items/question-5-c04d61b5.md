– A man deposited some amount in a bank on compound interest for 4

years at the rate of 20% per annum. The difference between the amount
accumulated after 4 years and the amount accumulated after 2 years is Rs. 12672. If
the same principle amount is deposited at simple interest at rate of interest r% per
annum for 4 years, then find the value of ‘r’, if the simple interest obtained by man is
Rs. 4000.
FUB – Answer r = 5
