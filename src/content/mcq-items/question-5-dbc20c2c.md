–A statement is given followed by three arguments. Decide which of the

Arguments is/are strong with respect to the statement.

Statement:

It is observed that in India, many students go to foreign countries for studies, and in
the end, they settle there.

Arguments:
1. Foreign countries have a better lifestyle.
2. Quality of education in foreign countries is much better than that in India.
3. Foreign countries give more privileges to their citizens.
