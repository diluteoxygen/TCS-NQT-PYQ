There are three taps A, B and C in the tank. These taps can fill the tank

in 10 hours, 20 hours and 25 hours respectively. At first, all three taps are opened
simultaneously. After 2 hours, tap C is closed and A and B keep running. After 4
hours, tap B is also closed. The remaining tank is filled by Tap A alone. Find the
percentage of the work done by Tap A itself.
