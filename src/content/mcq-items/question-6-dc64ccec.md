– The following criteria is decided by a company for the selection of an

Accountant.
1. The candidate must have done MBA in Finance
2. Minimum and maximum age of the candidate should be between 21 years and 35 years
As 31 March 2022.
3. If the candidate does not satisfy the above two conditions but has completed his/her CA,
the case will be forwarded to the Finance head and he/she can allow the candidate to
Appear for the interview.
