– A rabbit takes 9 jumps for every 5 jumps of a lion and fox takes 7

jumps for every 9 jumps of a rabbit. The distance covered by the lion in 3 jumps, the
distance covered by the fox in 2 jumps and the distance covered by the rabbit in 5
jumps is equal. Find the ratio of their respective speed.
