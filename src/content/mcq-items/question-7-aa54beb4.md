– There are five floors in a building, numbered 1 to 5 in such a way that

the lowermost floor is numbered 1 and the topmost floor is numbered 5. Five
members, namely R, L, T, C and A, live on different floors of the building, but not
necessarily in the same order. Also, these five work in different companies, namely
C1, C2, C3, C4, and C5. T stays on floor no. 2 and works in C4, and C stays on floor
no. 5. Also, the one who stays on floor no. 1 is working in C1. Who lives on floor no. 3
And in which company does she/he work?

Statements:
1. Neither L nor A works in C1.
2. One who lives on floor no. 3 works in C3; A does not work in C3.
